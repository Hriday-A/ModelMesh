Couples log in with Facebook accounts, choose from a variety of themed settings—including rooftop, beach, wheat field, and forest venues—all decorated with IKEA furnishings that guests can purchase with a click. For the ceremony to be legally binding, the couple, a marriage officiator, and two witnesses must be together in the same room to complete the necessary paperwork, though all other guests can attend remotely.
